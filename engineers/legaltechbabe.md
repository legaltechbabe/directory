### About Me

I'm a Senior Information Security professional working across security governance, risk, and compliance, from SOC 2 and ISO 27001 programs to vulnerability management and third party assurance. My background is in law and regulatory compliance, and I still lean on that foundation today. Understanding how a requirement is written tells me exactly where it tends to fall apart in practice.

That legal grounding is what pulled me into hands on GRC engineering. I've built ISMS frameworks, automated access reviews and evidence collection for audits, and worked with engineering teams to get security controls built into the pipeline instead of bolted on after the fact. I think the strongest GRC work happens when you understand the systems you're governing, not just the policy sitting on top of them, and that's the lens I bring to it. Outside my day to day work, I'm the WiCyS UK&I Community Lead and founded the Women in CyberSecurity chapter at the University of Surrey, which keeps me close to people just starting out in this field the way I once was.

Happy to connect with people working across law, compliance, and security engineering, or anyone finding their way into GRC without a traditional background.

### Experience Highlights

- Automated user access reviews and audit evidence collection using AWS security tooling, cutting manual work out of SOC 2 and ISO 27001 audit prep.
- Built and ran SOC 2 Type II compliance programs end to end, translating Trust Services Criteria into working controls and evidence auditors could rely on.
- Built and matured an ISMS aligned to ISO 27001, closing the gap between documented policy and how controls actually operate day to day.
- Ran vulnerability management programs, prioritizing remediation by exploitability and business impact rather than raw severity score.
- Partnered with engineering to embed SAST/DAST scanning and security controls into GitHub CI/CD pipelines, moving security checks earlier into the development lifecycle.
- Developed AI governance frameworks to assess security and risk for AI driven products, covering model risk, data handling, and responsible use controls.
- Led third party and customer security assurance reviews against SOC 2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS, and NIST aligned requirements.
- Delivered executive level security reporting, translating technical risk into decisions non technical leadership could act on.
